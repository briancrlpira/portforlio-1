about

Trained at the bench. Sharpened in the notebook.

I started in clinical biostatistics — designing trials, defending analysis plans, and learning exactly how much rigor a p-value actually earns. That discipline now shapes how I build machine learning systems: every model gets interrogated for confounding, leakage, and the difference between correlation and something you can act on.

Today I work across survival analysis, causal inference, and applied ML, partnering with clinical and research teams to turn messy longitudinal data into evidence that survives peer review — and production.
